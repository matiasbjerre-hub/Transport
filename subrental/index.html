Missing items & Setup Cost from a stock export

Upload the stock/availability export (.xls/.xlsx) for an order. Every item with Missing stock > 0 is listed below with its Setup Cost, converted to DKK.

How this is calculated: Setup Cost = quantity × Setup cost (€, from the master data) × 7.46038 (fixed EUR→DKK peg rate). Item numbers starting with 19 are local Scandinavian articles that may legitimately be missing from the master data — those rows show 0 with a note rather than being flagged as an error. Any other item not found in the master data is flagged in red as a genuine data gap.
