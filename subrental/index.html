Upload the stock/availability export (.xls/.xlsx) for an order. Every item with Missing stock > 0 is listed, then downloadable as a ready-to-submit .xlsx for the internal subrental request.

Item numbers starting with 19 are local Scandinavian articles — these can only be subrented from our own warehouse, so they're excluded entirely rather than listed. Any other item not found in the master data is flagged in red (description shown as given in the stock export instead). Rental days defaults to the length of the subrental period above, but is editable per row.
